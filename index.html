Follow the calculation

Step 1: moles NaCl = 8.00 g ÷ 58.44 g/mol = 0.1369 mol
Step 2: 50.00 mL ÷ 1000 = 0.05000 L
Step 3: molarity = 0.1369 mol ÷ 0.05000 L = 2.74 M
Important: total solution volume ≠ volume of water added In lab, you will dissolve the NaCl first and then add water until the final solution volume reaches the calibration line on the volumetric flask. This beaker is only a visual model that lets you change the volume.
